How priorities are calculated

The pilot score uses 30 points for surface heat, 25 for lack of vegetation, and 15 each for lower income, the share of residents who identify as Black or Brown, and the share living on block faces without trees. Heat and income use percentiles of eligible areas. The other factors use their shares directly. The weights are a proposed planning policy for this pilot and should be reviewed with residents and partner organizations.

The race factor is an area-level equity measure. A sector needs data for all five factors, at least five valid satellite pixels and at least 80% valid pixel coverage to receive a score. Income is the median for household heads with earnings.
