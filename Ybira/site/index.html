How to measure

Measure the total sidewalk width excluding the curb. Enter how much clear walking space would remain at the planting point.

Use the highest column met in Table 03 of the municipal guide. Check distances to intersections, poles, utilities and existing trees on site.

How the ranking works

The guide's site conditions and the locally native species list determine which trees are eligible. Species listed in the Oct 27, 2026 nursery inventory rank first, then established use and listed stock count. The site shows up to four candidates.
