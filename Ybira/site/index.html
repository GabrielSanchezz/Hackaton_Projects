    How priorities are calculated

    The score uses 30 points for surface heat, 25 for lack of vegetation, and 15 each for lower income, the share of residents who identify as Black or Brown, and the share living on block faces without trees. Heat and income percentiles use the current scale: São Paulo districts or Brasilândia census areas. Compare scores within the same scale. The weights are a planning proposal for review with residents and partner organizations.

    The race factor is an area-level equity measure. An area needs all five indicators, at least five valid satellite pixels and at least 80% valid pixel coverage to receive a score. Income is the median for household heads with earnings.
